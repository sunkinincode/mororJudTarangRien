# Surface Brief: index.html

## Scope & Visitor Mode
- **Primary Target**: `index.html` (and `app.js`, `styles.css`)
- **Mode**: `operate`
- **Audience & Task**: PSU students across 5 campuses searching 4,039 real SIS courses (6,276 sections), adding custom subjects, dragging and dropping sections/time slots on a weekly light-table canvas, customizing calendar backdrops (themes + custom uploaded photos with opacity/blur controls), checking class/exam clashes, and exporting studio-grade PNG wallpapers (Landscape & Portrait IG Story).

## Direction contract

- **THESIS**: A backlit architectural drafting light-table where course blocks act as translucent lucite strips over user-curated photography or drafting stock, refusing generic SaaS dashboard cards.
- **OWN-WORLD**: Warm drafting vellum (`#f7f5f0`), deep blueprint ink (`#12263a`), anodized teal (`#0f766e`), amber warning (`#d97706`), crimson clash alert (`#e11d48`), precision hairline rules, and border-encoded block states (solid official SIS, dashed custom subject, doubled crimson clash).
- **STORY**: Students instantly search 4,039 PSU courses, drag blocks across time slots or alternate SIS sections with live conflict telemetry, dial in custom background layers, and export crisp schedule posters.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT**: Top anodized telemetry bar (Credit Budget Meter, Wayfinding Clash Banner, Add Custom Subject button, Export PNG button); left 340px catalog & section inspector; right interactive 7-day light-table timetable canvas with backdrop studio bar and exam/course ledger below.
